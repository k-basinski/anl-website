I’ve been part of the laboratory since 2023 and am now a PhD Candidate there. Within the lab, I lead my own research projects, handle participant testing, data analysis, and organizational tasks, and contribute to study design and manuscript writing. My master’s thesis, “Mismatch responses to varying levels of inharmonicity of complex sounds,” explored auditory perception and predictive processing mechanisms, and was later published in *Scientific Reports*. In 2025, I also received the Best Poster Award at the 22nd World Congress of Psychophysiology.

My research interests center on the physiological mechanisms of perception and sleep, as well as predictive processing and cognitive functions. I am also a Research/Teaching Assistant at the Medical University of Gdańsk (MUG).

Outside of academia, I love cooking, watching movies, and am a devoted cat owner 😊
